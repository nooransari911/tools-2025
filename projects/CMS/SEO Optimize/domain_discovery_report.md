## Methodology Overview

The domains below were discovered by filtering the daily GoDaddy expired domain auction dataset. We utilized a highly refined pipeline that completely strips out false positives and spam:

1. **Authority Filter:** `Semrush AS >= 5`
2. **Market Validation (Social Capital):** `Price > $1.00`. We discovered that domains sitting at a $1 bid are universally ignored by human investors and algorithms alike, making this the ultimate filter for weeding out toxic spam or heavily manipulated metrics.
3. **AI Safety Net:** An LLM passed over the remaining domains strictly to eliminate literal algorithmic gibberish (e.g., `jfksldjfksd.com`) while preserving foreign words, acronyms, and proper names.
